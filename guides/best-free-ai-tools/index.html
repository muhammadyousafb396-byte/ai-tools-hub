Frequently Asked Questions

Are free AI tools really free?
The tools listed here have genuinely usable free tiers or are open-source. Some have usage limits; all are free to start with no credit card.

What is the best completely free AI image generator?
Stable Diffusion — free, open-source, unlimited, runs on your own computer. Leonardo AI and Ideogram are the best free hosted options.

Can free AI tools be used commercially?
Often yes, but check each tool’s terms. Leonardo AI allows commercial use on its free tier; others vary.

Why do AI tools offer free tiers?
To let you experience the value before paying. Free tiers are marketing — but smart users get real work done on them.
